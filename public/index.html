What This Means

Emergency Fund and Debt Load use simplified proxies — average recent spend stands in for "essential" expenses, and Debt Load measures account balances rather than actual monthly payments. Classify your accounts under Account Classification in settings. These are a starting point for your own judgment, not financial advice.

Financial Independence iFIRE Number = Annual Expenses × (100 ÷ withdrawal rate) — the classic "4% rule" is a 25x multiple. % Reached = your net worth ÷ that number. Years to FI projects your current savings pace compounding at your Financial Insights return rate until net worth reaches the FIRE Number. There's no way to derive a personal FIRE target from your data — set your own Annual Expenses below, or leave it blank to use your trailing 12-month average spend, annualized.
