Take-home pay is set in Settings (auto from that month's real income unless overridden). Every spending category is tagged to one of the four buckets below under Category Classification — same pattern as Account Classification already uses for Emergency Fund, Investment, and Liability accounts. A category can belong to only one bucket.

A 401(k)/employer-plan contribution is withheld from your paycheck before it ever reaches a tracked account, so Actual has no transaction to show it from. Enter your monthly amount here and it's added to Take-Home Pay above and to the Investments bucket below — this page can't know when you've hit the yearly IRS limit and contributions stop, so it assumes the same amount every month.

How the Targets Work

Percentages are of take-home pay (what actually lands in your accounts), not gross income — the same basis the book uses throughout. Fixed Costs running high isn't a one-month problem to fix; it usually means a structural cost (rent, a car payment) that only changes with a bigger decision. Guilt-Free running low isn't automatically good — it can mean spending is landing in uncategorized spots instead of getting classified, which is worth a look before reading too much into any single month.
