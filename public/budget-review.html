⚠ Every Dollar's Job

Gap is this category's trailing average monthly spend minus its current budgeted amount. LUMPY marks a category whose spend swings wildly month to month (a sinking fund — an annual premium, a planned purchase) — its Gap reflects that it's saved for over time, not chronically over budget, so read it as "check the pattern" rather than "fix the number." Planned is a trial figure you can edit per row; nothing here changes Actual Budget's own category budgets.
